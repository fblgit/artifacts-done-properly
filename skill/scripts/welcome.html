What it is for

01

Bring it in

Give good work a home. Bring interfaces, records and artifacts into a project with your agents. Make the useful parts discoverable instead of burying them in a chat.

02

Make it reusable

Keep the mechanics. A kit carries more than pixels: an interface, declared dependencies and examples another agent can inspect and compose.

03

Keep the gain

Build on your best version. A growing catalog gives you more to start from; refinement becomes an investment you carry across pages and projects.

What is included

  • The adp skill and four road skills: new, catalog, edit and adopt, which say how to design, build and edit a page.
  • Three page templates: starter, assistant and default.
  • The runtime adp.js: What changed, content as data, comment anchors, page integrity and first-open seeding.
  • The scripts adp.py, which prints the Artifact calls for the agent to make (it publishes nothing itself), and a local emulator, adp.py dev.
