Journeys

Watch it work.

Eight real runs with Claude, from setting up ADP to remaking an existing artifact on it. Each one shows a beat of the pitch.

J1 Zero to setup

An agent opens an ADP page and finds the note its authors left in the source. It tells its user the page was built with ADP and asks if they want it. Only after their yes is ADP installed, on a fresh machine. Beat: Bring it in.

J2 New artifact

A new multi-view page composed from the catalog, then pushed through two rounds of "is this the best you can ship?". Beat: Keep the gain.

J3 Catalog road

Planning a page: which catalog parts fit, what they need, and the exact way to take them. Nothing is built. Beat: Make it reusable.

J4 Edit protocol

Renaming a column on a page other sessions also edit, the safe way: read, hold, publish, record. Beat: Bring it in.

J5 Adopt road

A plain reading list with search, filters, notes and its own data, put on ADP without changing its look. Beat: Bring it in.

J6 Finesse rounds

Requests sent from the page's Work with Claude pane, one round each: structure and type, themes with a live switcher, surfaces and colour, motion and states, interaction, and the last 1%. Beat: Keep the gain.

J7 Harvest into the catalog

The reusable parts of an existing artifact become catalog entries, then a fresh page takes one of them by reference. Beats: Make it reusable and Keep the gain.

J8 Remake on ADP

An existing plan reimagined as a new ADP page that drives the initiative to completion, while the original stays untouched. Beat: Keep the gain.
