01 / Bring it in

Give good work a home.

Bring interfaces, records, and artifacts into a project with your agents. Make the useful parts discoverable instead of burying them in a chat.

Every piece gets an address

On ADP, every piece of a page is a part: a block with a stable id, such as triage.keys-table, and a hash of its content. The id never changes once the page is published, so anything that points at it keeps pointing at the right place.

That is what makes the page a place to work in rather than a file to replace. A comment anchors to a part id, not to a position on the screen. A change shows up on the part it touched and on every part that contains it.

Every change goes on the record

One ArtifactData layout records every change under a read, hold, publish, record protocol. Before anyone edits, they read the latest version, because a copy from an earlier session may predate other people's work. After they publish, the change is recorded with who made it, the parts it touched and a summary. The page shows the newest of those in its What changed panel.

  • hold

Your agents work in it with you

The assistant template adds a Work with Claude pane. Pick a block, say what you want (a change, a fix, a design note or a question) and send it. The request is a record on the page, so you can follow it on Requests:

  • queued
  • seen
  • working
  • done

A line in the header says whether a Claude Code session is watching. Without one, Ask now still answers straight away, and anything you send waits in the queue.

Start new, or bring in what you have

A new page starts from a template:

python3 -I adp/scripts/adp.py new assistant my-page

An artifact you already have goes through the adp-adopt road. It wraps each view and section in part markers, checks them, and seeds the record, without changing how the page looks or behaves. That first step alone gives the page part ids and comment anchors.
